# 60–90 second real demonstration plan

1. **0:00–0:10** Film the phone showing the welcome screen. Explain: "Outside Lab is designed to make you leave the screen."
2. **0:10–0:20** Check in with a real mood and select a 3-minute courtyard session. Show the timer starting.
3. **0:20–0:30** Set the phone down. Show a short real-world video of the activity, using a separate camera if convenient. Do not imply 10 seconds of video are a completed 3-minute session.
4. **0:30–0:45** Return after the actual elapsed time; complete the before/after rating, and record one observation.
5. **0:45–1:05** Show the real journal entry and call Backboard for the next AI experiment.
6. **1:05–1:25** Show the real model suggestion and explain how session history was included in the request. Note uncertainty.
7. **1:25–1:30** Close with "The model's next question depends on what I actually did outdoors."

## Safety and honesty
- Use a safe enclosed courtyard or another accessible outdoor area; no need to expose your phone in public.
- Turn off unrelated notifications before recording.
- Never show API keys, passwords, exact home GPS, private addresses or personal health information.
- Don't fabricate outdoor sessions or AI results.
- No promise of medical effects or automatic proof someone was outdoors.
